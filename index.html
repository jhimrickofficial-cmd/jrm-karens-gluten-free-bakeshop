Welcome to Karen's

Made for the Love of Good Food.

Good food brings people together. At Karen's Gluten Free Bakeshop, we believe enjoying something delicious should always be a moment worth looking forward to.

Located in beautiful Sedona, Arizona, our bakeshop offers gluten-free sweet treats, breads, savory favorites and special-order cakes. Stop by for a little indulgence, discover something new, or pick up something to share.
